What the lab sees

Four measures decide most of your plan. We read them on the treadmill, the bike, in the flume or on court, then again at every retest.

Breath gas, blood lactate, force plates and video at 240 frames a second. The report is yours the same day, in plain words and in the raw files.

A week at Arcline

Sessions run before work and after it, so training fits a life instead of replacing it. Most members mix three sports in one week.

  1. Monday

    Retest and easy spin

    06:30 · Lab and bike room

    A short read of last week's data with your coach, then forty easy minutes.

  2. Tuesday

    Track intervals

    06:30 · Outdoor track

    Threshold repeats paced from your lab numbers, in groups of four.

  3. Wednesday

    Strength and mobility

    18:00 · Gym floor

    Heavy, short and technical: the lifts that protect a season, coached rep by rep.

  4. Thursday

    Pool technique

    07:00 · 25 m pool

    Stroke video on the deck, drills in the water, one change at a time.

  5. Friday

    Rest, sauna and cold

    Any time · Recovery suite

    The day the training turns into fitness. Book the plunge or just sleep in.

  6. Saturday

    Long ride or long run

    08:00 · From the club

    Steady hours with a coach on the bike, coffee at the turn.

  7. Sunday

    Court drills and review

    10:00 · Courts 1–3

    Footwork and serve work, then ten minutes on the week ahead.

Before your first test

Still unsure? Call the club and a coach will answer, usually the one you'll train with.

Do I need to be fit already?

No. The first test sets your starting point, whatever it is. Most members start from three sessions a week or fewer.

What happens in the lab test?

Two hours: a short interview, a ramp test on a treadmill, bike or in the flume with breath gas and lactate readings, a gait or stroke video and a strength screen. You leave with a report the same day.

Can I train for more than one sport?

Yes. Plans are built from one engine profile, so a runner who also plays tennis gets one week that serves both.

How often do I retest?

At the end of every block: twelve weeks on Base and Build, eight on Peak.

Can I pause my membership?

Any time, for injury, travel or a new baby. Your data and your plan wait for you.

Is there parking and a shower?

Bike racks, ten parking spaces and showers with towels at the club.
